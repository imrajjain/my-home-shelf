A quiet place for what you want to read, watch and remember.

Shelf keeps books, films, shows, papers and places in one calm Mac app. Share what you choose with friends; keep the rest to yourself.

Download for Mac

Or install from Terminal, and skip macOS's “unidentified developer” step:

curl -fsSL https://gentle-water-02c5bb500.5.azurestaticapps.net/install.sh | bash

With Homebrew:

brew install --cask imrajjain/tap/shelf
Shelf runs on macOS 14 or later and updates itself. If you download it directly, the first time you open it go to System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
