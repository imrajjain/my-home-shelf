Friends

Share a shelf, not your whole life.

Pick a @username. Follow people to see what they share with everyone, or become friends to see what they share with friends. Each shelf — and any single item — can be Only me, Friends, or Everyone.

  • Everything starts private. Sharing is a choice, never a default.
  • Notes, ratings and progress stay yours.
  • See what friends finished lately, and add their picks to your own shelf in one click.
  • A public page anyone can open — no app needed.

The Museum

Rooms to wander through.

Get Shelf

Free, for Macs with macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. Once installed, Shelf keeps itself up to date.

Terminal easiest

Downloads, installs and opens Shelf — no security prompts.

curl -fsSL https://shelf.rajjain.fyi/install.sh | bash

Homebrew

If you already use Homebrew.

brew install --cask imrajjain/tap/shelf

Download

Unzip, move to Applications. The first time, open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
